Tier 2

What is in each

SampleScene

The game — 40 × 40 ring · 10 boxers · full presentation
  • GameLifetimeScope — binds every model, system and view; installs the single ring itself because there are no arena children
  • Ring — Walls, Floor, Posts, SpawnPoints, Boxers — the ring dressing; walls carry BoxCollider2D but do not contain anyone — positions are model-driven and clamped in BoxerSystem
  • CameraRig — CinemachineCamera + Brain + ImpulseListener — SpectatorCameraView writes the transform directly and frames one exchange, not the whole field
  • CombatFeedback — hitstop, impulse shake, five particle systems, impact lights, all audio
  • MatchHud / PlayerStatusHud / Standings / RosterCard / DiagnosticsHud — five UIDocuments; layouts in UXML, styling from porumble.uss
  • RingLighting — house lights down and key light in as the field thins
  • KnockoutMood — blends a desaturated grade and the mixer's Knockout snapshot
  • MatchInput — the restart key

Training1v1

Curriculum stage 1 — 20 × 14 ring · 2 boxers · spawn radius 4.5 · auto-restart
  • GameLifetimeScope — same bindings; every presentation view is simply absent and injected optionally
  • BoxerSpawnPoints — boxerCount 2, scriptedBoxerIds [1] — one learner against the hand-written sparring brain
  • 2 × BoxerView — pre-placed rather than instantiated, so the ring is visible without entering play mode
  • no HUD, no camera rig, no feedback — a countdown would burn episode steps on animation

Training10

Curriculum stage 3 — 40 × 40 ring · 10 boxers · spawn radius 15 · auto-restart
  • GameLifetimeScope — one ring, installed at the root
  • Ring/SpawnPoints — ten spawn transforms dealt round a circle, rotated and jittered every episode
  • Boxer_Template — an inactive scene copy; the spawner instantiates from the prefab asset
  • DebugInputView — missing script — the referenced script no longer exists; logs a warning on every run. Pre-existing, harmless, worth deleting

Training10x8

Parallel training — generated — 8 rings, 80 units apart · 80 agents · auto-restart
  • GameLifetimeScope — registers only the shared half — config, roster, ratings — and skips the per-ring install because arena children exist
  • 8 × Arena_NN → ArenaLifetimeScope — each owns its own MatchModel, BoxerSystem, MatchDirector and message brokers
  • 8 × Ring — identical copies, offset on a 3-column grid
  • spacing 80 — the ring is 40 across and RayLength is 24, so anything under 64 lets a fighter perceive the fight next door
