The Card

Six fighters share one 119,313-parameter network. What separates them is a style that bends the network's actions and four physical attributes. This page compares what they are specified to be — see the caveat below before reading it as performance.

This compares specification, not measured results. No per-fighter win rate exists yet: the checkpoint evaluator (PoRumble ▸ Evaluate Checkpoints…) has not been run, and the shipped policy cannot load against the current observation space anyway. Until a measurement run happens, treat every chart here as "how they are built", not "how well they do".
