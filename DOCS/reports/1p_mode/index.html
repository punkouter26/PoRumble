1 Player mode - UI changes

2026-10-10. Captured from the Editor's play view, which stretches the portrait layout into a 1280x720 frame; proportions on a phone differ, positions do not.

Title screen

  • 1 PLAYER starts the fight with you in the STANDARD RL seat.
  • DEMO is the old FIGHT button: all nineteen seats are AI.
  • The two buttons share one row, so the screen is no taller than before.

In the fight

  • The player's boxer is black and plain, in the seat STANDARD RL holds in the demo.
  • The camera is pinned to the player, exactly centred, while they are standing; after a knockout the broadcast camera takes over.
  • The field strip marks the player's row in blue.

Not shown: the player's HEALTH / BREATH / POWER panel. It appears on a desktop; where a touchscreen is present (including the Editor's Device Simulator, used for these captures) the existing touch layout hides those bars.
