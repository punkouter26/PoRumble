Joint drive

Six revolute DOF per fighter. No ArticulationBody, no ConfigurableJoint, no SLERP — a 2D hinge has no orientation to interpolate.

The servo is a P controller, and its error must wrap. HingeJoint2D.jointAngle is cumulative and never wraps at 180°, so a raw target − jointAngle runs away once a joint gets round: measured mid-fight at −6543° asking for 300,000 °/s, gloves nine units from their own torso and never recovering. Mathf.DeltaAngle plus an 1800 °/s clamp is the fix.

The card

One network drives all six. StyleModulator bends the actions it produced on the way to the boxer — so they share a brain and differ in how they use it. Bars are magnitude on one hue; the coloured chip is the fighter's ring colour, carrying identity only.

Chin is inverted: it multiplies damage taken, so BIGGIE at 0.70 is granite and BLIPPI at 1.40 is glass. The aim is never bent — pointing at an opponent is the one thing the network is genuinely good at, and rotating its output produces a worse fighter rather than a different one.
